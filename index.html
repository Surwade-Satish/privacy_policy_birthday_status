2. Google Play Developer Guidelines Adherence

To ensure total transparency for both our users and Google Play review teams, we explicitly affirm that our application complies with all Google Play developer standards:

🛡️ No Dynamic Code Loading (DCL)

In adherence to Google Play System Integrity policies, this application contains no external executable code loading (.dex, .jar, or .so files from remote sources). All code is verified and built statically within the signed application package.

🔒 Zero-Permission Media & Storage Access

We do not request broad device storage permissions (such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES). Media selection for birthday photos is handled via the zero-permission Android Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickVisualMedia), ensuring the app only touches photos you explicitly select.

👥 Least-Privilege & Transparent Address Book Access

Contacts permission (READ_CONTACTS) is purely optional. The app features a dual contact picker architecture allowing users to either pick individual contacts via the zero-permission system picker or grant contact read access solely to import names, birthdays, and photos locally into the Room database with upfront rationale disclosures.

⏱️ Compliant Foreground Service Specification

Our background birthday monitoring service explicitly specifies the dataSync foreground service type (android:foregroundServiceType="dataSync") under Android 14+ (API 34+) requirements, displaying an active ongoing notification with complete user control to stop or pause the monitor at will.

🎯 Transparent AdMob Monetization & No Deceptive Ads

Advertisements rendered through Google AdMob comply with Google Play's Ads and Monetization Policies. We avoid unexpected interstitial popups, do not simulate system dialogues, provide clear ad labelling, and respect user privacy through Google Mobile Ads SDK consent mechanisms.

3. Information We Collect and Process

We collect and process only the minimal data strictly necessary to provide the app's core birthday countdown and status-sharing functionality:

  • Phone Number: Used exclusively for secure verification and login identity authentication via One-Time Password (OTP). Your phone number is never shared, leased, or sold to third-party telemarketers.
  • Birthday Entries: Names, dates of birth, relationship classifications (e.g. Family, Friend, Colleague), and optional personal notes you choose to create.
  • Selected Images: Contact or profile photos you capture with your camera or select from your gallery to generate customized 9:16 birthday celebration banners.
  • Local Contact Data (When permitted): Address book names, phone numbers, and birth dates extracted at your direct request when using the Contact Import feature.
  • Diagnostic & Device Telemetry: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic logs, device OS versions, and performance metrics collected via Google AdMob and standard Android system APIs to ensure stability.

4. On-Device Storage & Data Security

We prioritize local-first data architecture:

  • Local Room SQLite Database: All birthdays, countdowns, reminders, and custom notes are stored 100% locally on your device in a sandboxed Room database.
  • Private Storage for Photos: Any photos assigned to birthdays are saved inside the application's internal files directory (context.filesDir), inaccessible to other apps installed on your device.
  • No Unauthorized Cloud Sync: Your personal contact lists and birthday celebrations remain entirely on your device.
  • Encryption in Transit: Any network communication (such as OTP verification requests or Google AdMob network calls) utilizes industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).

6. Detailed Permissions Justification

POST_NOTIFICATIONS FOREGROUND_SERVICE READ_CONTACTS CAMERA VIBRATE RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to deliver morning birthday alerts and reminders.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC: Allows the app's optional Live Database Monitor to check upcoming dates and maintain notification alerts.
  • READ_CONTACTS (Optional): Used solely when you click "Import Contacts" to load names and birthdays into your app.
  • CAMERA (Optional): Used only if you choose to take a real-time portrait photo for a birthday card.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Reschedules local notification alarms when your phone restarts.

7. User Control, Data Deletion & Account Erasure

We respect your Right to be Forgotten and comply with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy:

  • Delete Individual Birthdays: You can edit or delete any birthday entry at any time directly from the list.

  • Delete Account: Tapping "Delete Account" in the app's Settings clears all user preferences, wipes the database, deletes saved photos, and resets login tokens permanently.
